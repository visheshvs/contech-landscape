# ConTech Landscape

An open, weekly-updated directory of technology companies across the **AECO ecosystem** (architecture, engineering, construction and operations), including property tech. It covers design and engineering tools, preconstruction and estimating, bidding and procurement, project controls, field operations, construction finance, offsite construction, supply chain, property and asset management, smart buildings, sustainability, workforce and safety, and AI and data platforms.

- **Browse:** https://visheshvs.github.io/contech-landscape/
- **Data:** [`data/companies.csv`](data/companies.csv) and [`data/companies.json`](data/companies.json)
- **What changed:** [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md)

## Add your company

Email **visheshvikramsingh@gmail.com** with the subject line:

```
[ConTech Landscape] Company submission: <Company name>
```

The [website](https://visheshvs.github.io/contech-landscape/#submit) has buttons that open a pre-filled email or copy the template. Or paste this template:

```
Company name:
Website:
LinkedIn page:
HQ (city, country):
One- or two-sentence description of what you do:
Category (pick 1-3, from the list below):
Subcategory (e.g. Quantity takeoff, Bid & tender management):
Customers (e.g. General Contractor, Architect, Owner / Developer, Trade - Electrical):
Project phases (Planning & Feasibility / Design / Preconstruction / Construction / Operations):
Latest funding (round, amount, date), if public:
Your name and role:
```

Submissions are checked against the company's own website and LinkedIn page before they're added. Descriptions are written in plain language and aren't copied from marketing copy.

## Fields

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `company` | Company name |
| `description` | One or two plain-language sentences on what they do |
| `category` | One or more high-level categories (list below) |
| `subcategory` | One or more specific product types (list below) |
| `customer` | Who buys or uses it (list below) |
| `phases` | Project phases served: Planning & Feasibility, Design, Preconstruction, Construction, Operations |
| `funding` | Most recent public funding (round, amount) |
| `hq` | Headquarters (city, country) |
| `website` | Company website |
| `linkedin` | Company LinkedIn page |
| `date_added` | Date added to this directory (YYYY-MM-DD) |

### Subcategories
- **Design & Engineering:** BIM authoring; Model coordination & clash; Generative & automated design; Engineering analysis; Permitting & code review
- **Preconstruction & Estimating:** Site selection & feasibility; Conceptual estimating; Quantity takeoff; Detailed estimating; 5D / model-based estimating; Cost data & benchmarking; Preconstruction platform
- **Bidding & Procurement:** Bid & tender management; Subcontractor prequalification; Procurement & quoting; Proposal generation
- **Project Management & Controls:** Project management suite; Cost management & controls; Change order management; Scheduling & planning; Risk & forecasting; Documents RFIs & submittals
- **Field Operations & Jobsite:** Reality capture & progress; Inspections & QA/QC; Daily logs & field reporting; Equipment & fleet; Construction robotics software
- **Construction Finance & Payments:** Payments & pay apps; Lending & draws; Construction accounting & ERP; Insurance & surety
- **Offsite & Industrialized Construction:** Prefab & modular
- **Supply Chain & Materials Commerce:** Materials marketplace; Logistics & delivery
- **Property & Asset Management:** Facility & maintenance; Property management; Digital twin
- **Real Estate Development & Investment:** Development & underwriting
- **Smart Buildings & Facilities:** Building systems & IoT
- **Sustainability & Compliance:** Energy & carbon; Embodied carbon & LCA; Regulatory & compliance
- **Workforce & Safety:** Safety & EHS; Workforce & labor; Training
- **Data AI & Integration Platforms:** AI agents & copilots; Data integration & APIs; Analytics & BI
- **Residential & trades:** Residential builder software; Trade business management

### Customers
- **Owners and operators:** Owner / Developer; Owner's Rep / PM; Investor / Lender; Public Agency; Facility / Property Manager
- **Design:** Architect; Structural Engineer; MEP Engineer; Civil Engineer; Interior Designer
- **Builders:** General Contractor; Construction Manager; Design-Builder; Homebuilder; Remodeler
- **Trades:** Trade - Electrical; Trade - Mechanical / HVAC; Trade - Plumbing; Trade - Fire Protection; Trade - Concrete; Trade - Steel; Trade - Drywall / Interiors; Trade - Roofing; Trade - Glazing; Trade - Sitework / Civil; Trade - Painting / Finishes; Trade - Other
